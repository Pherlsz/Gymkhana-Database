Privacy Policy — Toca do Gamba

Last updated: 2026-09-26

Who we are

Toca do Gamba (“the app”) is an internal operations tool used by authorized staff. Contact: tocadogamba.database@gmail.com.

What data we collect

  • Google account identity used for sign-in: email address, display name, and profile photo URL provided by Google OAuth.
  • Operational records entered by signed-in users (for example people profiles, documents, bills, and uploaded attachments).
  • Session cookies required to keep you signed in securely.
  • Basic technical logs (request identifiers, timestamps, error codes) used to operate and secure the service. We do not sell personal data.

How we use data

Data is used only to authenticate allowlisted operators, provide the application features, maintain auditability, and keep the service secure. Access is limited to authorized roles inside the app.

Sharing

We use infrastructure providers (hosting, database, object storage) as processors to run the app. We do not share personal data with third parties for advertising.

Retention

Account and operational records are retained while the organization needs them for operations and compliance. Session cookies expire according to the app session lifetime. You may request deletion of your operator account by contacting the email above.

Your choices

Only allowlisted Google accounts can sign in. To revoke Google access, use your Google Account permissions settings. For questions or requests about personal data in the app, contact the email above.
